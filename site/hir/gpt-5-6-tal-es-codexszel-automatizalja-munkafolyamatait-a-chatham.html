GPT-5.6-tal és Codexszel automatizálja munkafolyamatait a Chatham

A Chatham Financial az OpenAI modelljeivel 30 percről 4 perc alá csökkentette a tranzakciók ellenőrzéséhez szükséges időt.

A Chatham Financial pénzügyi tanácsadó cég az OpenAI Codex és GPT-5.6 modelljeit integrálta a mindennapi működésébe. A fejlesztéseknek köszönhetően a tranzakciók ellenőrzési ideje 30 percről 4 perc alá csökkent. A vállalat egy saját belső platformot is létrehozott, ahol a munkatársak egyedi, a munkájukat segítő alkalmazásokat építhetnek.

Az alkalmazások alapértelmezetten a GPT-5.6 Terra modellt használják, de a bonyolultabb feladatokhoz a GPT-5.6 Sol verzió is elérhető. A cég emellett a Chatham Onyx nevű operációs rendszerében is alkalmazza a mesterséges intelligenciát, amely segít a piaci adatok elemzésében és a jogi dokumentumok kezelésében.

A Codex modellt a szoftverfejlesztési ciklus teljes egészében használják a tervezéstől a tesztelésig. Bár az automatizáció jelentős, a Chatham szakértői továbbra is személyesen ellenőrzik a kimeneteket a pontosság érdekében.

Chatham Financial uses Codex and GPT‑5.6 to build technology and redesign workflows, cutting trade validation from 30 minutes to under 4. Chatham Financial⁠(opens in a new window) advises clients on complex capital markets decisions, where accuracy and auditability are paramount. As AI capabilities advanced, the firm saw an opportunity to turn proven processes into governed, repeatable workflows and software, reducing the time spent on routine execution, so its experts can focus more on client needs. Today, Chatham uses Codex to build internal and client-facing tools and GPT‑5.6 to power AI features. This work spans employee-built applications, its next-generation capital markets operating system Chatham Onyx, and its reengineering consulting service Process Zero, which accelerated trade validation from 30 minutes to under 4. Through Process Zero, Chatham reengineers workflows around outcomes. For each workflow, the firm identifies the minimum inputs and evidence required, determines where human judgment is essential, and decides how AI and AI-built tools should support the work. Trade validation is an early example. Chatham’s Controls and Data Integrity team protects the accuracy of transaction data by validating that each system record reflects what the client authorized and what was executed. Using Codex, Chatham developed a trade validation application that gathers supporting transaction evidence, compares key terms, and flags discrepancies for review. Chatham is comparing the app’s results with those of experienced reviewers. Chatham plans to extend the application to additional trade types and automate more of the workflow while maintaining appropriate controls and professional oversight. Chatham’s client-facing AI work grew from its own operating experience. Chatham’s employees use ChatGPT and Codex in research, analysis, drafting, software development, and other daily activities. AI has become a practical tool widely adopted across the firm. Employees have also become builders through Chatham Vibes, an internal platform for creating applications tailored to their work. Employees build with a range of models, and the AI features inside Vibes applications run on GPT‑5.6 Terra by default, with GPT‑5.6 Sol as an optional upgrade based on per-app configuration. These applications increasingly support client-facing workflows. One helps teams review maturing-cap trades and prepare pricing workbooks and client communications. Others support workflows such as producing fixed-income rate sheets, preparing hedging dashboards, and reviewing trade confirmations. Chatham professionals bring the market context, client understanding, and judgment to evaluate the work, refine it where needed, and determine what reaches the client. Chatham Onyx brings assets, debt, and derivatives into an environment where clients and advisors can work from connected, governed data and use AI without losing traceability to the underlying source. Chatham uses Codex across the Onyx development lifecycle. Teams use it to help plan, build, test, document, and review software, shortening the path from a concept to a durable product capability. The Onyx platform leverages GPT‑5.6 Sol, GPT‑5.6 Terra, GPT‑5.4, and GPT‑4.1. Tasks like simple analysis and non-production testing are routed to the most cost-effective models, while GPT‑5.6 is used for complex tasks that need to maximize accuracy and value. One example is ChatFIN, which summarizes patterns in historical market data, helps users understand their portfolios, and locates and links to legal documents for debt, derivative, and lease terms. These capabilities help clients and advisors access information and insights, while Chatham’s advisors bring the subject matter expertise needed to interpret that information and apply it to each client.
