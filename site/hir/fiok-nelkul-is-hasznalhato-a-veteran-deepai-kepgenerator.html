Fiók nélkül is használható a veterán DeepAI képgenerátor

A 2016 óta működő DeepAI regisztráció nélkül kínál ingyenes képgenerálást, de a képminőség elmarad a modern versenytársak mögött.

Kipróbálom: DeepAIdeepai.org

A 2016-ban indult DeepAI az egyik legrégebbi fogyasztói AI-eszköz, amelynek legnagyobb előnye, hogy fiók létrehozása nélkül is lehetővé teszi a képgenerálást. Az ingyenes verzióban a felhasználók teljes kereskedelmi jogot kapnak az elkészült képekre, így azok szabadon felhasználhatók.

A szolgáltatás ingyenes változata ugyanakkor csak alacsony felbontású képeket készít, a jobb minőségű generálásokért pedig előfizetést kér. A havi 9,99 dolláros Pro csomag nagyobb felbontást és több funkciót ad, de a tesztek szerint a DeepAI képminősége így is elmarad a modern modellek mögött, mivel a fejlesztők régóta nem frissítették a mögötte álló motort.

Ha professzionális marketinganyagokhoz keresünk alternatívát, a Krea ingyenes csomagja napi 100 számítási egységet biztosít a Krea 2 modell használatához. Emellett a Leonardo.Ai és az Ideogram is jobb minőségű képeket kínál, bár ezeknél már kötelező a regisztráció.

Az eredeti szöveg (Krea)
DeepAI has been online since late 2016, which makes it one of the oldest consumer AI image tools still running — it predates Midjourney, DALL·E 2, and Stable Diffusion by years. That history is exactly why it shows up in searches today: people land on deepai.org, see a plain interface that looks unchanged from an earlier era of AI, and want to know whether it is still worth using, what the free plan actually gives you, and what has replaced it since. This guide answers all three, with every number checked against DeepAI’s own pricing and tool pages this week, plus three current alternatives — Leonardo.Ai, Ideogram, and Krea — so you can decide where to generate your next image. Every figure in this post comes from a page we loaded on October 5, 2026: DeepAI’s pricing page, its Text to Image tool page, and its about page, Leonardo’s Tokens FAQ, Ideogram’s plans and billing docs, and Krea’s own pricing page. For the popularity question specifically — “how many people use DeepAI” — we checked three independent traffic estimators (Similarweb, Semrush, and Ahrefs) rather than inventing a single number, because none of these tools publish an audited user count and the estimators disagree with each other by a wide margin, which we report plainly below instead of averaging it away. Every image in this post is a single, raw Krea 2 generation from the exact prompt printed beside it — made to illustrate the ideas in this piece, never to imitate a DeepAI, Leonardo, or Ideogram screenshot. A raw Krea 2 generation illustrating “old tool, new tools” — not a real screenshot of any product. DeepAI describes itself as “the all-in-one creative AI platform built for everyone.” Per its own about page, it “got our start in late 2016 with the first browser-based text-to-image generator,” which predates the current wave of consumer AI image tools by several years. Since then it has added an AI chat assistant with live web browsing, a photo editor, video generation, music generation, and a voice chat mode, all accessible from the same account. The core product people search for is still the Text to Image generator: type a description, pick a style, and get an image back. DeepAI’s own tool page states “No Sign-Up Needed: Use all core features immediately — just enter a prompt and go,” which is the main reason the tool still gets traffic — most competitors gate even a first generation behind an account. The page also states that users retain “100% of the rights to everything you create, even on the free plan,” and that outputs carry no copyright restriction on commercial use. Beyond the consumer product, DeepAI’s about page says it also takes on “custom technical projects for governments, nonprofits, and research organizations,” building computer vision systems for things like conservation and environmental monitoring under what it calls an “AI for Good” initiative. That side of the business is unrelated to the image generator most people land on the site for, but it explains why deepai.org also hosts a large library of machine-learning research paper summaries — a leftover from its original identity as a research-aggregation site before the image generator became its main draw. Yes, with a real, no-account path: you can generate images on deepai.org without creating an account at all. That is a genuine point of difference from Leonardo.Ai, Ideogram, and Krea, which all require a free sign-up before your first generation. What the free tier does not give you is DeepAI’s higher-quality resolution tiers. The Text to Image page lists four output tiers: Standard (the free default), HD at 640×640, Genius at 1024×1024, and Super Genius at full 2K — and the three higher tiers are gated behind DeepAI Pro.
